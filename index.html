RESEARCH PAPER

Published

Colorectal Cancer (CRC) Detection Using Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)

Authors: Sombit Pramanik, Snehasish Biswas, Soumyadeep Jana, and Asish Mitra

Published in: Journal of Mechanics of Continua and Mathematical Sciences (journalimcms.org)

This research uses Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to assess the likelihood of colorectal cancer by analyzing images of cancerous and non-cancerous tissue. The study reports strong precision, recall, and accuracy, and uses data augmentation and early stopping to improve generalization and reduce overfitting. Its findings highlight the potential of machine learning to support diagnostic accuracy and help oncologists make informed treatment decisions.
